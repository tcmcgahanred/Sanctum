## NEWS

**20260810-A — CISA warns of increased threat activity targeting water and wastewater control systems.** CISA reported a significant increase in threat actors targeting programmable logic controllers (PLCs) in the Water and Wastewater Systems sector, and urged operators to remove publicly exposed PLCs and other operational technology (OT) from the internet. Observed activity included attackers changing PLC passwords to lock out operators and altering device IP addresses, resulting in boil-water notices and forced manual operations. CISA noted water entities of all sizes are being targeted, including those with mature security programs, and specifically flagged exposed cellular modems.
*Source: CISA, "CISA Urges Water and Wastewater Systems Sector to Protect OT Against Activity Targeting PLCs," July 30, 2026. https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/07/30/cisa-urges-water-and-wastewater-systems-sector-protect-ot-against-activity-targeting-plcs*

**20260810-B — Coordinated cyberattack reported against Minnesota water utilities.** Security vendor Tenable published analysis of a reported coordinated cyberattack affecting Minnesota water utilities. Details of scope and impact require verification against the primary reporting before use.
*Source: Tenable, "Coordinated 'cyberattack' on Minnesota water utilities: What you need to know," July 28, 2026. https://www.tenable.com/blog/coordinated-cyberattack-on-minnesota-water-utilities-what-you-need-to-know*

**20260810-C — CISA warns Iranian threat actors targeting Siemens and Schneider industrial systems.** CISA warned that Iranian-linked threat actors are targeting Siemens and Schneider Electric industrial control systems. Affected-product and mitigation specifics require verification against the CISA advisory before use.
*Source: Infosecurity Magazine, "Iranian Hackers Target Siemens and Schneider Industrial Systems, CISA Warns," July 23, 2026. https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/iran-hackers-siemen-schneider-ics/*

**20260810-D — CISA warns of credential exposure affecting ~74,000 Fortinet devices ("FortiBleed").** CISA reported that malicious actors targeted internet-accessible Fortinet devices across government and private organizations using compromised credentials, in activity referred to as FortiBleed involving leaked credentials associated with approximately 74,000 Fortinet devices, including firewalls and VPN gateways. CISA urged hardening of affected devices.
*Source: CISA, "CISA Urges Hardening Fortinet Devices After Reports of Credential Exposure," June 18, 2026 (updated June 22, 2026). https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/06/18/cisa-urges-hardening-fortinet-devices-after-reports-credential-exposure*

## CTA TTPs

**20260810-E — Threat actors locking out water operators by seizing exposed PLCs.** In the water-sector activity CISA reported, threat actors gained access to internet-exposed PLCs and changed device passwords to lock out legitimate operators, then changed device IP addresses to disconnect them — degrading operations and forcing manual control. The technique requires no advanced tooling and depends on the target device being reachable from the internet.
*Source: CISA, "CISA Urges Water and Wastewater Systems Sector to Protect OT Against Activity Targeting PLCs," July 30, 2026. https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/07/30/cisa-urges-water-and-wastewater-systems-sector-protect-ot-against-activity-targeting-plcs*

**20260810-F — Attackers abusing RMM "take control" features after N-central authentication bypass.** CISA added an N-able N-central authentication-bypass flaw (CVE-2026-18577) to its KEV catalog after reports of active exploitation. Successful exploitation grants administrative access to N-central servers, after which attackers can abuse the product's built-in remote "Take Control" feature — a supply-chain risk for any organization whose IT provider uses the platform.
*Source: The Hacker News, "CISA Adds Exploited N-able N-central Flaw to KEV After Customer Compromises," August 4, 2026. https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cisa-adds-exploited-n-able-n-central.html*

## LATEST ATTACKS OR RISKS

**20260810-G — CISA adds three actively-exploited flaws to KEV (Langflow, Tomcat, N-central).** On August 5, CISA added three vulnerabilities to its KEV catalog citing active exploitation: CVE-2026-9198 (CVSS 9.8), an unauthenticated remote code execution flaw in Langflow on default deployments; CVE-2026-34486, a missing-encryption flaw in Apache Tomcat allowing a bypass of its EncryptInterceptor (patch deadline August 7, 2026); and the N-able N-central authentication bypass. Organizations running any of these should prioritize patching.
*Source: The Hacker News, "CISA Flags Langflow RCE, Tomcat, and N-central Flaws as Actively Exploited," August 5, 2026. https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cisa-flags-langflow-rce-tomcat-and-n.html*
*Source: CISA, "CISA Adds Three Known Exploited Vulnerabilities to Catalog," August 4, 2026. https://us-cert.cisa.gov/ncas/current-activity.xml*

**20260810-H — Active exploitation of Microsoft SharePoint Server vulnerabilities; CISA urges hardening.** CISA urged SharePoint hardening following new exploitation, and Tenable published analysis of three actively-exploited SharePoint Server vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-32201, CVE-2026-45659, CVE-2026-56164). On-premises SharePoint is common in local government and education; affected organizations should apply vendor guidance and review exposure.
*Source: CISA, "CISA Urges SharePoint Hardening After New Exploitations," July 14, 2026. https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/07/14/cisa-urges-sharepoint-hardening-after-new-exploitations*
*Source: Tenable, "CVE-2026-32201, CVE-2026-45659, CVE-2026-56164: FAQ About Active Exploitation of Microsoft SharePoint Server Vulnerabilities," July 16, 2026. https://www.tenable.com/blog/cve-2026-32201-cve-2026-45659-cve-2026-56164-faq-sharepoint-server-exploitation*

**20260810-I — Critical MikroTik RouterOS flaw exposes VPN private keys.** CISA published an ICS advisory for MikroTik RouterOS (CVE-2026-14227) in which low-privilege API access can extract the router's WireGuard private key in plaintext, enabling VPN impersonation and decryption of associated traffic. Low-cost MikroTik routers are common in budget-constrained environments; affected operators should update and restrict API access.
*Source: CISA, "MikroTik RouterOS," ICS Advisory ICSA-26-211-01, July 30, 2026. https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-26-211-01*

**20260810-J — Critical Check Point SmartConsole authentication bypass exploited in the wild.** Rapid7 reported active in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2026-16232, a critical authentication-bypass vulnerability in Check Point SmartConsole. Organizations running affected Check Point management components should prioritize vendor mitigations.
*Source: Rapid7, "CVE-2026-16232: Critical Check Point SmartConsole Authentication Bypass Exploited in the Wild," July 23, 2026. https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-cve-2026-16232-critical-check-point-smartconsole-authentication-bypass-exploited-in-the-wild*

**20260810-K — Apache Tomcat encryption flaw actively exploited; patch deadline set.** CISA added CVE-2026-34486, a missing-encryption vulnerability in Apache Tomcat (CWE-311), to its KEV catalog, citing active exploitation and setting an August 7, 2026 mitigation deadline. The flaw allows attackers to bypass Tomcat's EncryptInterceptor.
*Source: Cyber Security News, "CISA Warns of Apache Tomcat Encryption Vulnerability Actively Exploited in Attacks," August 5, 2026. https://cybersecuritynews.com/apache-tomcat-encryption-vulnerability/*
